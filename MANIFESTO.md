# The Personal GRIP Manifesto

## The Personal GRIP is an act of self-authorship.

It exists to restore the individual’s right to speak, create, remember, and publish without asking permission.
It is not a platform, not a product, not a marketplace. It is a personal space on the web, owned and governed by its author alone.

## 1. Sovereignty

The GRIP belongs to its owner.
No external authority decides what is acceptable, visible, promoted, ranked, or silenced.
The owner is the final arbiter of content, visibility, and meaning.

## 2. Freedom of Expression

The GRIP defends uncensored self-expression.
There is no algorithmic moderation, no shadow banning, no incentive shaping through metrics.
Speech is free by default; restraint, if any, is a conscious choice of the owner.

## 3. Authorship Over Performance

The GRIP values writing, thinking, and creating over performing.
Metrics such as views, likes, replies, or reach are optional and opt-in.
Nothing exists to hijack attention or distort expression for engagement.

## 4. Time Is Real

What is written happened.
The past cannot be rewritten or erased through convenience.
History is immutable, but its exposure is a choice: the owner decides what parts of the past are visible, to whom, and when.

## 5. Local First, Network Optional

The GRIP must run locally, independently, and offline if needed.
Networking, federation, and correspondence are extensions — never requirements.
The GRIP does not depend on a central service to exist.

## 6. Simplicity Over Scale

The GRIP rejects growth as a goal.
It favors clarity, durability, and understandability over scale, virality, and optimization.
A GRIP should still work decades from now.

## 7. Human Pace

The GRIP respects human rhythm.
No infinite feeds, no urgency loops, no artificial scarcity.
Interaction happens when humans choose to engage, not when software demands it.

## 8. Extensible, Never Captured

Plugins may exist, but they serve the owner — not advertisers, trackers, or platforms.
No extension may override ownership, history, or agency.

## 9. A Different Web

The Personal GRIP is not here to compete.
It is here to change the shape of the web by example:
from platforms back to people,
from feeds back to voices,
from performance back to presence.
